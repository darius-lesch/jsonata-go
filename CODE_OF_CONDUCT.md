# Code of Conduct

## Our Goal
We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive environment for everyone, regardless of background, identity, or experience level. We expect all contributors and participants to treat others with empathy, keep discussions constructive, and communicate professionally.

## Enforcement
Harassment, abusive language, or unacceptable behavior will not be tolerated. If you encounter or witness inappropriate behavior, please report it to the project maintainers. Project maintainers reserve the right to remove comments, issues, or access from anyone who violates these standards.
